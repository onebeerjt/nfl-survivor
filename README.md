# NFL Survivor Guide 2026

Every remaining game (Weeks 6–18) with each team's win probability from ESPN's
Matchup Predictor, in one tap-friendly board:

- **Grid** — all 32 teams × Weeks 6–18, color-coded by win probability
- **Burn tracker** — tap teams you've already used; they drop out of every recommendation (saved in your browser)
- **Best picks panel** — top 5 available teams per week, with "save value" warnings when a team has an even easier game later
- **Week headers** — each week shows its best available pick given your burned teams
- Tap any cell to lock it as your planned pick for that week

Data is a snapshot: run `python3 build_data.py` and re-inject to refresh
(probabilities move weekly). Built by Luna for JT, October 15, 2026.
